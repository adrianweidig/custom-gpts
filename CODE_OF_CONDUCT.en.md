# Code of Conduct

Languages: [Deutsch](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

This repository should enable factual, respectful and productive collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- communicate kindly and respectfully
- connect criticism to specific files, statements or changes
- account for different levels of experience
- name domain risks clearly without devaluing people
- do not publish confidential information

## Unacceptable Behavior

- personal attacks, insults or degrading comments
- harassment, discrimination or intimidating communication
- publication of private or confidential information
- intentional addition of secrets, malicious code or misleading security promises
- repeated disregard of clear maintainer guidance

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, close or remove issues, pull requests or comments that violate these rules. Repeated or severe violations may lead to exclusion from collaboration.

## Reporting

Report problematic behavior through an appropriate GitHub channel. Security-relevant content does not belong in public issues; use [`SECURITY.en.md`](SECURITY.en.md).
